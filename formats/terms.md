# Terms: <program name>

> Research Program Charter format `terms` (spec: `spec/terms.yaml`). Reader: the sponsor and the team. Updated: rarely; by the amendment rule it contains.
> Delete these guidance lines as you fill the sections in.

## 1. Purpose

> One sentence. Points to the thesis.


## 2. Sponsor and authority  *(required in the charter)*

> Who signs, who funds, who can end it. What the program may decide on its own. Who approves a version bump.


## 3. People  *(required in the charter)*

> Names, roles, time fractions. Investigators named against questions where that is real.


## 4. Envelope  *(required in the charter)*

> Total resources, not line items. The share reserved for untraced work.


## 5. Term and review  *(required in the charter)*

> Start, end date or end condition, the one review cadence.


## 6. Reporting  *(required in the charter)*

> What the sponsor sees, and how often.


## 7. Openness  *(required in the charter)*

> What is published, what is commercial, who decides, and the default when nobody has decided.


## 8. Judging  *(required in the charter)*

> How research continuation is judged. How product expansion is judged. Kept separate.


## 9. Endings  *(required in the charter)*

> Graduate, hand off, stop: the condition that triggers each.


## 10. Stewardship  *(required in the charter)*

> Who inherits the space, the open problems, and the tree when the program ends.


## 11. Amendments

> How the charter changes, and what forces a new version.


## 12. Signed

> Names and date.


---

Checks before it goes out:

- [ ] someone outside the team can tell who can stop the program and on what grounds
- [ ] time fractions add up to the people actually available
