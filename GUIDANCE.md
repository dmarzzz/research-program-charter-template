# Using the format

Advice, not structure. None of this is part of the spec.

## How much to write

The format is the same at any scale. Only the amount of writing changes.

| | Small team (roughly 3 to 8 people) | Large program (many teams, outside performers) |
|---|---|---|
| First version | 2 to 3 pages in about two person-days: one person drafts, the team red-teams it for 90 minutes, half a day of edits | each part drafted by its owner, then reconciled |
| Full charter | 6 pages at most | 10 to 15 pages |
| Standalone documents | only when a named reader asks for one | space, thesis, open problems and roadmap are normally all standalone |
| Extras | none | the optional call step, technical areas under the thesis, a separate evaluation performer |
| Upkeep | under two hours per person per month | owned by a program office |

The one-pager is the primary artifact at any scale. The charter is its appendix.

## Is it working?

Check after 90 days.

Working:
- the file was edited at the review without anyone chasing
- each person can name the question their current work answers
- it killed or reordered at least one thing
- an outsider cited a question id
- upkeep stayed within the budget above

Failed: a slide deck has become the real plan.
